# Privacy policy

For the [Braille Translator (English)](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/braille-translator-englis/kgngiapafahaekaelnejkmkjmcnkhmfj) Chrome extension. Last updated November 19, 2026.

## The short version

The extension does not collect, store, sell or share any of your data. Everything it does happens in your browser.

## What the extension does with web pages

To translate a page into braille, the extension reads the text on the pages you visit and replaces it with braille characters. The translation runs inside the extension, on your computer, using a bundled copy of [liblouis](https://liblouis.io/). Page text is never sent anywhere, and the extension makes no network requests at all.

## What the extension stores

It saves two settings with Chrome's extension storage, on your device only:

- which braille grade you picked, or off
- whether to show the original text next to the braille

Removing the extension deletes them.

## What the extension does not do

- No accounts, analytics, tracking, ads or cookies
- No browsing history or personal information is collected
- No data is sent to the developer or anyone else

## Permissions

- **Read and change data on websites you visit:** needed to swap page text for braille
- **Storage:** needed to remember your settings

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. The extension's source code is in this repo, so you can check what it does.
